# Kettle & Pine customer policy

## Shipping
- Orders ship from Portland, Oregon within 1–2 business days.
- Standard shipping (USPS): 3–7 business days in the US. Express (UPS): 1–3 business days.
- Free standard shipping on US orders of $75 or more (before tax). Otherwise standard shipping is $6.90 and express is $12.90.
- Canada: 7–14 business days, $19.00 flat. We do not ship elsewhere yet.
- Address changes are possible while an order is "processing". Once it has shipped we cannot change the address, but the customer can redirect it with the carrier.
- Local pickup is available at our Portland workshop for orders still "processing".
- If tracking shows "delivered" but nothing arrived, ask the customer to check with neighbours and wait 48 hours, then we open a carrier claim and reship.
- If tracking has not moved for 7+ days, we open a trace with the carrier and offer a reship or a refund.

## Returns and refunds
- Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Customer pays return shipping unless the item is our mistake.
- Damaged, defective, wrong or missing items: we cover everything. We send a free replacement or a full refund, the customer chooses. Ask for a photo for damaged items.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after the return is received.
- Orders can be cancelled for a full refund while still "processing".
- Grinders and kettles have a 2-year warranty against defects.

## Billing
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay and PayPal. Prices are in USD.
- Duplicate charges are refunded immediately once confirmed in our payment system.
- Card declines sometimes leave a pending authorization; it disappears within 3–5 business days.
- Promo codes cannot be added after an order is placed, but we refund the difference for valid codes within 7 days of purchase.
- Price drops within 14 days of purchase: we refund the difference once per order.
- VAT/company invoices and receipt copies can be issued on request from the order page or by support.
- Coffee subscriptions can be paused or cancelled any time before the next billing date.
- Business orders over $500 can pay by invoice with net 30 terms after approval.

## Tone
- Warm, short, specific. Use the customer's first name. Never blame the customer.
- Never promise anything outside this policy. If unsure, say a teammate will follow up.
- Reply in the customer's language.
